The transition plan converts discovery into an implementation sequence.

The transition work organizes each fund bucket into a proposed destination account and identifies how assets should transfer, what can move in kind, what must wait for maturity and where policy or board decisions need to be settled first.

Step 1

Define operating cash

Agree working balance, QIPP / IGT reserve needs and sweep mechanics before moving deposits.

Step 2

Update policy

Confirm investment policy, statutory requirements, authorized investments and reporting expectations.

Step 3

Open fund accounts

Separate reporting by fund bucket so each pool of capital can be reconciled and governed on its own.

Step 4

Transfer in sequence

Move agency positions in kind, wire pool balances, and handle CDs at maturity or confirmed renewal.

Scalable model

The scalable model is expertise, process, and relationship access.

Both practices are resource-intensive because much of the value comes from specialized discovery, governance work, and direct engagement. The opportunity is to add resources, process design, and capital support without diluting the high-touch approach that creates credibility.

01

Market identification

Prioritize hospitals and corporate prospects where Balefire's expertise solves a visible gap.

02

Discovery system

Standardize the intake model for statements, policies, decision makers, cash needs and open questions.

03

Governance toolkit

Build repeatable IPS, committee, board and counsel-facing materials by state and client type.

04

Advisor capacity

Support the specialists with operating leverage, transition management and reporting resources.

05

Expansion motion

Create deliberate handoffs into retirement plans, insurance, executive benefits and Private Wealth.

The opportunity is not to make the work generic. The opportunity is to make the best parts of the work repeatable, supported, and easier to scale.
